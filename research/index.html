Current Projects

Racial Disparities in Cesarean Delivery

I have been working with Dr. Ellen Kauffman and Dr. Kathleen Kerr on the topic of the existing racial disparity within delivery methods. With the goal to locate where intervention efforts may be most warranted, our focus has been to provide meaningful insights into the racially disparate cesarean rates using the CDC NVSS natality microdata.

Over the past seven months, we have analyzed the racial disparity in cesarean and induction rates within an NTSV population, including the evaluation of these disparities by differing comorbidity burden. By stratifying into subgroups of race and comorbidity burden, our results aimed to evaluate the robustness of racial disparity in the context of comorbidity. Similarly, the consideration of disparate induction rates alongside the disparate cesarean rates may identify potential avenues of clinical intervention. This research has recently moved into the submission phase.

Trial of Labor After Cesarean

We are also currently working on further research regarding trial of labor after cesarean (TOLAC) success rates among differing races and regions. The scope of our work with TOLAC is smaller, and uses the CDC NVSS aggregate dataset instead of the microdata due to regional variable restrictions.
