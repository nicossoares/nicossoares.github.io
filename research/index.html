Research

My recent research focuses on disparities in women's health, with a particular focus on maternal health outcomes using CDC NVSS datasets.

Racial Disparities in Cesarean Delivery

I have been working with Dr. Ellen Kauffman and Dr. Kathleen Kerr on racial disparities in delivery methods using CDC NVSS natality microdata.

Our work examines racial disparities in cesarean and induction rates within an NTSV population, including evaluation of these disparities across differing levels of comorbidity burden.

By stratifying by race and comorbidity burden, we aim to evaluate the robustness of observed racial disparities in the context of maternal health characteristics. Considering induction rates alongside cesarean rates may also help identify potential avenues for clinical intervention.

This research has recently moved into the submission phase.

Trial of Labor After Cesarean

We are also investigating trial of labor after cesarean (TOLAC) success rates across racial and regional groups. This work uses the CDC NVSS aggregate dataset because of restrictions on regional variables in the public-use microdata.
